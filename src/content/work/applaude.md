## Context and problem

Small teams often ship without knowing how their app will hold up: which screens break, which API calls fail, and whether the database copes when real users arrive. Finding out after launch is expensive.

## Approach

Applaude evaluates three layers of an application: the frontend UI, the backend APIs and the reliability of the database. It reports the issues it finds and gives an assessment of whether the app is ready for scale.

The pages it practises its website checks on are public and contain no real data.
